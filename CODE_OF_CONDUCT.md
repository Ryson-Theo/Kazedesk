# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Kazedesk to be a welcoming, respectful space for everyone who participates, regardless of background, identity, experience, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and respectful.
- Discuss ideas and work; do not attack people.
- Accept feedback graciously and focus on what is best for the project.
- Respect different perspectives and lived experiences.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, and other conduct that would make participation unsafe or hostile are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct that violates this policy to the repository maintainers privately through a contact channel listed on the repository or maintainer profile. Reports will be reviewed and handled as fairly and confidentially as possible. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary.

This policy applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-related communications.
